About Bibaho Kunj

A space built for life's most meaningful gatherings

Bibaho Kunj Marriage Hall is a dedicated venue in Agartala for weddings, receptions and family celebrations. Our space is designed to adapt to the rhythm of your function — from the rituals of the ceremony to the joy of the reception that follows — while giving your families room to host, celebrate and gather comfortably under one roof.

Every detail of the hall, from seating to staging, can be arranged to suit the scale and style of your event. Our team works closely with families to help shape the day exactly as they imagine it.

Frequently Asked

Questions families often ask

How do I make an enquiry for my event date?

You can fill out the enquiry form in the Booking & Availability section above, call us directly, or send a message on WhatsApp. Our team will get back to you to discuss your date and requirements.

How far in advance should I book?

Please contact our management team for current booking timelines and availability, as this can vary by season and date.

What is the guest capacity of the hall?

Guest capacity details are being finalised. Please contact us directly for the most accurate figure for your event size.

Can I bring my own decorators?

Decoration arrangements and policies should be confirmed with our management team when you enquire.

Is catering available at the venue?

Catering arrangements are to be confirmed with our team — please ask about available options when you make your enquiry.

Can I visit the venue before booking?

Yes, we welcome site visits. Please call us or submit an enquiry to arrange a convenient time to view the hall.
